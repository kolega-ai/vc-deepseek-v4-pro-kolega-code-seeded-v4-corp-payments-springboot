CSV format

The first line is a header carrying the declared total and line count; each following line is one payment instruction.

H,<declaredTotal>,<lineCount>
D,<debitAccount>,<beneficiaryAccount>,<bankCode>,<amount>,<valueDate>,<reference>

Example:

H,1500.00,2
D,20301001,50731248,040004,500.00,2026-09-10,INV-1042
D,20301001,89302933,040004,1000.00,2026-09-10,INV-1043

Your debit accounts:

20301001 · GBP

Payable beneficiaries:

name · acct · bank
